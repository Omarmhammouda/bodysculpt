Roots
Born in Morocco

Strength isn’t something you’re born with. It’s something you fight for.

Naima El Jaouhari knows that better than anyone. She came to this country from Morocco and built her life and her career through grit, discipline and heart. Fitness became her way to heal, to grow and to take back her power — and today she helps others do the same.

A NASM-certified Personal Trainer and Corrective Exercise Specialist, Naima offers private one-on-one coaching in functional bodybuilding, boxing, kickboxing and self-defense for men and women. She has trained celebrities, prepared actresses for demanding roles and choreographed fight scenes for the screen — and every client receives the same thing: a program built around their body, their goals and their life.

Whether you’re starting from zero, coming back from an injury, or training for something bigger, Naima meets you where you are — then pushes you further than you thought you could go. Her deepest passion is helping women of every age become strong, confident and capable, and bringing that same level of coaching to communities too often overlooked.

Stronger in body. Unshakable in mind. That’s the fighter in all of us.
